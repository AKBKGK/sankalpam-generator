NEW IN 5.2.2 – NO SILENT DEFAULTS, NAME ENDINGS, LIVE PREVIEW, SHARE
(Requested after a first-time-user test on a phone, 9 Oct 2026.)
  ✓ Birth star starts blank ("– Select birth star –") and must be chosen before
    Generate or Subscribe. Earlier, an untouched form recited Aśvinī / Meṣa.
    A note asks the user to confirm the star. Pāda and rāśi wait for the star.
  ✓ Family members: the birth star also starts blank and is required when a
    name is entered.
  ✓ Gotra: new "Not known (use Kāśyapa)" choice, with a short note.
  ✓ Name ending: None · नाम्नः (new default), Śarmā, Varmā, Gupta and Dāsa are
    all available in the saṅkalpam (no longer "coming soon"); moved out of the
    fold next to Gender; note "Choose the ending your family uses. If unsure,
    keep None." and a "Why?" box quoting Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.10.9 with a link.
    Women keep नाम्न्याः. The tarpaṇam stays Śarmā-only (message explains).
  ✓ Name box: "Your first name only (no surname)", example "shreenivaasa"
    (was "raama daasa", which looked like a name plus an ending).
  ✓ Live preview under the name: the kartā's line of today's saṅkalpam in the
    chosen script, updating while typing; ____ marks anything missing.
  ✓ Share button (phone share sheet), shown where the phone or browser has one.
  ✓ Email box under the saṅkalpam: takes the address to the Daily email tab.
  ✓ Home-screen icon opens like an app (manifest "display": "standalone").
  ✓ Vākya stays the default; the Vākya / Tirukkaṇita switch moved into
    "Edit pañcāṅgam".
  ✓ Welcome box: "A saṅkalpam is a vow: you state when, where, who and why
    before a pūjā." (highlighted; also in the one-line version).
  ✓ "📍 Use my location for sunrise times" under City: asks permission only
    when pressed, picks the nearest city in the list and says how far it is.
  ✓ Name, gotra, star, pāda and ending are remembered as soon as they are
    entered (earlier: only on Generate); typed names are saved while typing.
  ✓ "Not known (use Kāśyapa)" is remembered, with its note.
  ✓ Tarpaṇam with a non-Śarmā ending: a "Use Śarmā and continue" button
    instead of a dead end.
  ✓ Mahālaya tarpaṇam is offered only from 15 days before Mahālaya pakṣa until
    its last day (next: 1–30 Sep 2027 by the app's calculation; the pakṣam
    itself 16–30 Sep 2027). On other dates the box starts at the tarpaṇam of
    the day, or Amāvāsyā. Owner link to see it on any date (this device only):
    https://sankalpam.live/?show=mahalaya  – and ?show=off to hide it again.
  ✓ Unchanged: pañcāṅgam calculations and all tarpaṇam text.
  ⚠ Waiting for the vādhyār: वर्मणः, गुप्तस्य, दासस्य, नाम्नः, नाम्न्याः in the
    saṅkalpam; Kāśyapa for an unknown gotra.
  ✓ Daily email: no change to Code.gs needed (it loads the live app code);
    run refreshEngine() after uploading – see README.txt.

NEW IN 5.0
──────────

1. VĀKYA PAÑCĀṄGAM (traditional) – now really calculated
   ✓ Step 1 "Calculation method": Vākya (traditional) or Tirukkaṇita (modern
     astronomical). Before 5.0 both options used the astronomical calculation.
   ✓ Vākyakaraṇa method: Vararuci's 248 candravākyas, Vākya Sun table and the
     Vākyakaraṇa saṅkrānti formula; calibrated to printed Vākya pañcāṅgams
     (true sunrise, about 11–13°N, Tamil Nadu).
   ✓ Tithi, nakṣatra, yoga and karaṇa at sunrise, with "upto" times.
     Clock times follow the printed convention: 6:00 AM + nāzhigai × 24 min.
   ✓ The method used is highlighted under step 1 and in every footnote.

   Tests against print (2026):
     Tithi (29 days)                average 2.2 min, worst 5.5 min
     Nakṣatra (3 seasons, 79 days)  average 1.4–2.1 min, worst 7 min
     Saṅkrānti                      within 1 minute
     Tamil month starts             11 of 11 correct
     Tirukkaṇita vs published Drik  within about 1 minute

2. "WHICH DAY?" – Tarpaṇam tab
   ✓ Amāvāsyā: next Amāvāsyā by the aparāhṇa (afternoon) rule, afternoon
     window highlighted, "close call" warning.
   ✓ Mahālaya pakṣam: dates and Mahālaya Amāvāsyā; each tithi on request.
   ✓ Māsa pirappu: next saṅkrānti time, the day the month begins, next 12 months.
   ✓ Compare Vākya and Tirukkaṇita. "Use" fills in the date (and month).
   ✓ Only one Tarpaṇam box (at the top of the tab).

3. KĀRUṆIKA PITṚS
   ✓ The Add button now works; one relative per click.
   ✓ Relationships grouped: father's side, mother's side, others.
   ✓ Gotra filled from the relationship (your gotra / your mother's), or chosen.
   ✓ Devanagari name fills in; lines grouped by gotra in the text.
   ✓ Men: …गोत्रान् … शर्मणः वसुवसुस्वरूपान् कारुणिकपितॄन् …
     Women: …गोत्राः … नाम्नीः वसुवसुस्वरूपाः कारुणिकपितॄः …
   ✓ "Atthai" corrected to father's sister; language box removed.

4. OTHER
   ✓ Tamil spellings → Sanskrit stems for men's names
     (Chidambareshwaran → चिदम्बरेश्वर, Subramanian → सुब्रह्मण्य,
      Kalyanaraman → कल्याणराम, Srinivasan → श्रीनिवास).
   ✓ Abhivādaye Veda and Sūtra follow the tarpaṇam choices (no second entry).
   ✓ The tarpaṇam suggested for a date no longer overrides your own choice.
   ✓ One combined disclaimer; "independent of any publisher or maṭha".
   ✓ Personal name removed; MIT License.
