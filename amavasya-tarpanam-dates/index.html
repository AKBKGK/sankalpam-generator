Vākya or Tirukkaṇita?

Vākya is the traditional method of printed Tamil pañcāṅgams, worked out for India; its times are Indian time. Tirukkaṇita is modern astronomy, worked out for the sunrise and clock of the city you choose. In India and Sri Lanka, follow the pañcāṅgam your family uses. Outside India and Sri Lanka the app uses Tirukkaṇita automatically, because it follows your own sunrise.
