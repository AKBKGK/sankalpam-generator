Saṅkalpam Generator

Prepare a complete saṅkalpam with today's pañcāṅgam, your details and the pārāyaṇams you intend to chant.

▼ Most fields on this page are drop-down boxes – tap a box and choose. Items left on Skip are not included. Boxes you change are highlighted, and your choices are listed in the bar at the bottom.

1Date & Pañcāṅgam

Choose the date. The pañcāṅgam fills in automatically for sunrise in India (IST); correct any field if your pañcāṅgam differs.

2Kartā (Person Doing the Saṅkalpam)

Type the name in English; the Devanagari fills in automatically and can be corrected. For long vowels type double letters (e.g. shreenivaasa → श्रीनिवास).

⚠️ Please check your Rāśi.

3Śrī Rudram

P = full pārāyaṇam · G = ghanam of one anuvāka · P + G = pārāyaṇam (full or one anuvāka) and ghanam of an anuvāka.

4Sūktas, Sahasranāmams & Aṣṭottaras

Leave everything on Skip for a simple daily saṅkalpam.

Vedic Sūktas – Skip / P (Pārāyaṇam) / G (Ghanam) / P + G
Sahasranāmams – 1000 names – Skip / Pārāyaṇam / Arcana (no anuvākas, no ghanam)
Aṣṭottaras – 108 names – Skip / Pārāyaṇam / Arcana

5Purpose / Prayers (optional)

Common English words (health, wealth, peace, success, education…) become the Sanskrit term; other words are converted letter by letter (kshema → क्षेम). Check the Devanagari before generating.

6Samāpanam (Completion)

Choose how the saṅkalpam should close.

In "Detailed", Vedic closings (आभिर्गीर्भिः…, शं नो मित्रः…) are added when Namakam, Chamakam or any Vedic Sūkta is selected.

Fill in the Generator tab and press Generate Saṅkalpam.
