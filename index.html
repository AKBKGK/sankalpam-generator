Rāhu kālam, Yamagaṇḍam and Kuḷigai are the fixed weekday timings printed in Tamil calendars (6 AM sunrise convention); actual local times move a little with sunrise. Festival days use the common rules – Ekādaśī at sunrise, Pradoṣam at sunset, Vināyaka Caturthī at midday, Saṅkaṭahara Caturthī at moonrise, Amāvāsyā and the śrāddha tithi by the afternoon (aparāhṇa) rule. Observances can differ by family and sampradāya, so please confirm with your pañcāṅgam or vādhyār.
