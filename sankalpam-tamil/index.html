What is a saṅkalpam?

Saṅkalpa (सङ्कल्पः) means a firm resolve. Before a pūjā, homa or tarpaṇam you state when (the day's pañcāṅgam), where, who (your name, gotra and star) and why. The app fills in the day for your city; you add your details once.

What the app fills in for you

  • The day's pañcāṅgam for your city: saṁvatsara, ayana, ṛtu, māsa, pakṣa, tithi, vāsara and nakṣatra
  • Your name, gotra, birth star and rāśi, and your family members if you add them
  • The purpose and closing you choose, and Vākya or Tirukkaṇita calculation

The Tamil months in your saṅkalpam

The Tamil calendar is solar. Each month begins when the Sun moves into the next rāśi (the saṅkrānti, kept as māsa pirappu). In the saṅkalpam the month is said by its rāśi name, not its Tamil name. That is why the example above, for a day in Kārttigai, says vṛścika māse.

The year starts with Chithirai. In the Parābhava year, Chithirai 1 was Tuesday, 14 April 2026, the day of the Viṣu puṇyakālam.

On which day does the month change?

If the saṅkrānti falls before sunset, the new month begins that day. If it falls after sunset, the month begins the next day. For example, the Tulā saṅkrānti on Sunday, 18 October 2026 was at 6:13 AM (Vākya), so Aippasi began that day. The app follows this rule, so the month in your saṅkalpam changes on the right day. For a saṅkrānti at night, practices differ; please ask your vādhyār.

Vākya or Tirukkaṇita?

Vākya is the traditional method of the printed Vākya pañcāṅgams, used mainly in Tamil Nadu. The app uses it unless you change it. Tirukkaṇita uses modern astronomy, worked out for the sunrise and clock of your own city. The two methods' times are often about two hours apart, and on about one day in ten the tithi or star at sunrise is different. Choose the one your family's pañcāṅgam uses.

Questions

The calendar says Kārttigai, but my saṅkalpam says vṛścika māse. Is that wrong?
No. Kārttigai is the month when the Sun is in Vṛścika, and the saṅkalpam names the month by the rāśi.

Which tithi and star does the saṅkalpam use?
The ones that prevail at sunrise on that day.

Does the month also change the ṛtu and ayana?
Yes. In the Tamil calendar the ṛtu, the ayana and the year follow the solar month, so the app changes them together.

Can I use it outside Tamil Nadu?
Yes. Choose your city. With Tirukkaṇita, the times are worked out for your city's sunrise; Vākya times follow Indian time, like a printed Vākya pañcāṅgam.
